# Aerolytica

**Your bridge to autonomy.**

Aerolytica is an independent drone technology consultancy connecting drone
technology with commercial reality. We help organisations source and deploy the
right technology, and help drone businesses reach new markets.

## Our portfolio

- **Technology sourcing:** Vendor-independent sourcing of drones, payloads,
  sensors and flight or data software.
- **Consultancy:** Practical guidance on technology selection, compliance and
  operational integration.
- **Bid and tender writing:** Support to help drone businesses identify and win
  opportunities.
- **Go-to-market and sales channels:** Market-entry strategy and channel
  development for drone companies.
- **Training and service provision:** Expertise to support effective drone
  operations.

We work across defence, government, public safety, agriculture, construction,
environment, education and commercial sectors, with a global network of
manufacturers, software innovators, distributors and industry specialists.
